How earning works

Points are calculated from the eligible amount of completed external transfers, purchases, and fee-paying services explicitly approved by MineBank. Account tier determines the configured rate.

  • Failed, pending, cancelled, and reversed activity does not earn points.
  • Transfers between your own accounts are excluded.
  • CashLine draws, credit repayments, loan activity, and banking corrections are excluded.
  • Reversals are recorded in your points history.

Only activity types enabled by MineBank's current rules can earn points.
