How CashLine works

Interest is calculated daily on used credit. The approved facility determines the maximum available amount.

  • Minimum request: 300 Emerald
  • Monthly service cost: 10 up to 5,000; 20 above 5,000
  • Personal annual rate: 18.3%
  • Business/Corporate annual rate: 23.7%
  • No early repayment fee

Illustrative service-cost calculator

Pricing principles

Own-account transfers are free. Transfers to other accounts are free up to 500 Emerald, then a standard 5 Emerald fee may apply unless the tier provides fee-free transfers.

Scheduled payment setup costs 2 Emerald and each execution costs 1. Payment service requests cost 1 Emerald.

Inside the secure MineBank banking portal. This institutional website never displays customer account data.

No. It is an informational calculator and does not access, submit or modify a customer account.
